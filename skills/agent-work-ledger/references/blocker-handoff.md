# Blocker or handoff

Use when work cannot continue normally or must pass to another executor. Verify blockers with the `SKILL.md` recovery budget.

For a blocker, record on the canonical surface when one exists: blocker, unblock condition, validation impact when applicable, remaining useful work, and exact next action. If determining the underlying cause or remedy requires substantive technical, product, design, or domain work, route that diagnosis or resolution to the appropriate executor; the ledger owns the blocker state, not the substantive answer.

If untracked shaping has no canonical surface, create or adopt an Initiative only when the blocker or handoff now requires durable tracking and that mutation is authorized; otherwise surface the blocker without manufacturing tracking. Continue independent useful work when appropriate; otherwise stop.

For a handoff, make the canonical surface sufficient to resume without prior chat: current durable state, blockers or constraints, validation when applicable, material decisions, pending substantive outcomes, and exact next action.

Use comments only when notification or durable history is needed beyond current state.

## Done when

The unblock condition and ownership of any required substantive resolution are explicit, or another executor can resume safely from durable state.
